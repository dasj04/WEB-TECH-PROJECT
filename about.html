ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT

Excellence in Technology, Innovation & Research

Who We Are

The Department of Information Technology, Gauhati University, is dedicated to providing high-quality education, cutting-edge research, and industry-aligned technical training. Established with a vision to empower students with modern technological skills, the department has grown into one of the most dynamic academic units of the university.

Our Vision

To become a center of excellence in Information Technology education and research, producing globally competent professionals and innovators.

Our Mission

  • Deliver strong foundational and advanced IT education.
  • Encourage innovation, creativity, and problem-solving.
  • Collaborate with industry for skill-oriented learning.
  • Promote research in emerging technologies.
  • Develop responsible and ethical IT professionals.

Why Choose Us?

Experienced Faculty

Highly qualified professors with strong academic and research backgrounds.

Modern Labs

Fully equipped labs for AI, Machine Learning, Cloud Computing, and Networking.

Industry Integration

Internships, workshops, and seminars with leading IT companies.

Research Focused

Opportunities for research projects, publications, and real-world problem solving.
