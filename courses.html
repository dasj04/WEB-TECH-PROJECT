Integrated Programmes

Integrated B.Sc. + M.Sc. in Software Development (Five Years)

Intake: 30 · Duration: 4 (UG) + 1 (PG)

Admission

Admission and counselling based on 10+2 marks. Counselling details will be notified at www.gauhati.ac.in.

Eligibility

10+2 with Mathematics at 10+2.

Programme Structure

Download Program Structure

Important Features

  • Students select one Major; change of Major allowed after two semesters based on merit and seat availability.
  • Two Minor subjects are taken in the first three semesters; Minors available across campus subjects.
  • Curriculum includes AEC, VAC, SEC, MDC, internships and skill-building components.
  • Exit points: after 1 year — Certificate; after 2 years — Diploma; after 3 years — B.Sc. (Major); after 4 years — B.Sc. (Honours) or B.Sc. (Honours with Research); after 5 years — Integrated M.Sc.
